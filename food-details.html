About this dish

Our signature Hyderabadi Chicken Biryani follows a multi-generational recipe perfected over 30 years. Every portion is slow-cooked on charcoal embers with whole spices like black cardamom, star anise, cinnamon quills, and shahi jeera to ensure the aroma permeates every grain of rice.
